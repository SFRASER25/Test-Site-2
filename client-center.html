Getting ready to file

Your document checklist.

You'll receive a personalized list through our outreach process — here's what most clients gather.

Income

  • W-2s and 1099s (NEC, INT, DIV, B, K)
  • Schedule K-1s from partnerships, S-corps & trusts
  • Brokerage and equity compensation statements
  • Rental income and expense summaries

Deductions

  • Mortgage interest (Form 1098)
  • Property tax and state estimated payments
  • Charitable contribution receipts
  • Retirement and HSA contributions

Changes

  • Asset purchases or sales
  • Moves, marriages, or new dependents
  • New businesses or entities
  • IRS or state notices received
