Our firm

Redefining what it means to be a CPA firm.

We started iTxRe on a simple idea: your income tax shouldn't be the largest unknown expense in your financial life. It should be planned, modeled, and fully understood — long before you file.

Accounting reimagined. Advisory redefined. Taxes, made simple.

Our approach

We combine an automated, outreach-based document process with forward-looking tax modeling — so compliance is effortless and planning is continuous.

Business owners and high-net-worth clients meet with us at least twice a year to review a custom tax model. When life changes, we plan for the impact in advance. The result is a relationship built on clarity, not surprises.
