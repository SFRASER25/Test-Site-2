Entity & Compensation Strategy

The way your business is structured — and the way you pay yourself — can matter more than any deduction.

01

Choosing the right entity

LLC, S corporation, partnership, or C corporation — each carries different tax, liability, and exit consequences.

Our approach: we model each option side by side using your actual numbers before you elect or restructure.

02

Reasonable compensation

S-corp owners must balance salary and distributions — too little invites scrutiny, too much wastes payroll tax.

Our approach: a defensible salary benchmark and distribution plan built into your model.

03

Retirement & benefit design

Solo 401(k)s, cash balance plans, and accountable plans can shift significant income out of today's bracket.

Our approach: we quantify each option's tax impact so you can decide with your financial advisor.
